A utility, not a chat tab.

Hotkey capture

Picture or text mode on the active window. Every shortcut is remappable — click the field and press the keys.

Your own Gemini key

Bring a key from Google AI Studio. Add several — the next one is used if a key is rate-limited or out of quota.

Runs in the tray

The background service shows up as Sync Host. Start, restart, or stop it from the tray or the app.

Settings stay local

Keys and shortcuts live in config.json on your PC. No account, no sync, no dashboard.
